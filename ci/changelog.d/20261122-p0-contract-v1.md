# P0: version 1 of the contract

- `seeddrift/train/rel/api.py` replaces the placeholder of the foundation commit: the vocabulary (73 tokens; a target
  is always a residue, tokens 1 to 65), the 19 blocks of a row of 1,024 slots (16 relation blocks of 32, two neighbours
  of 128, the target gene of 256, stored last), the kinds of a (row, block) with `KIND_NONE = 0`, the arrays of a shard
  and the arrays no model ever reads, the names of layouts and conditions (`condition()` joins combined ones with "+"),
  the planning formula of the cost per cell, and the columns of a result row. `API_VERSION` (the name the foundation
  reads) equals `API` = 1.
- `col` is a 0-based offset inside a block in the coordinates of the frame row (grid slot = block start + col);
  -1 marks a codon without a column.
- Not in the contract on purpose: the table-set manifest (`handoff.py`), the layout of shard files (one reader and
  writer, package W2) and the evaluation items, strata and result-row writer (one implementation, `evalh/`).
