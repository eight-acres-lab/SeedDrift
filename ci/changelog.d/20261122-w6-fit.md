Package W6 (with the minimal trainer of W4): the reference trunk and its training.

- `seeddrift/train/rel/models/axial.py`, `models/common/layers.py`: the axial trunk (row attention over the whole row
  with rotary position inside a locus on half of every head's dimensions; column attention among the rows of a table
  with a learned "no source" key; no row index), sizes 10m (8 x 256), 30m (12 x 384), 100m (12 x 768); self-tests in
  the module.
- `seeddrift/train/rel/data/`: the reader of table sets (`tables.py`, the stored placement), the sampler, the mask
  schemes `scattered`, `span`, `whole_gene`, and the plan of the held-out evaluation (`heldout_plan.py`, numpy only,
  shared with the baselines).
- `seeddrift/train/rel/objectives/mlm.py`, `trainer/`: bidirectional fill-in under the fixed protocol; the budget is
  cells seen or the wall limit, whichever ends first; held-out evaluation on the way and at the end, written as rows
  of the contract's result table.
- Stages `w6_selftest`, `w6_fit`, `w6_report` of kind `rel_traineval` (`ci/rules/rel_w6.smk`): the torch self-tests in
  the model interpreter with the GPUs hidden; every arm preflighted, then trained with the stage's own scheduling (a
  failed arm does not stop the others); the curves aligned by cells seen beside the classical baselines.
- Variants `configs/rel/variants/w6-ref10m`, `w6-ref30m`, `w6-ref30m-norel`, `w6-ref100m`; profiles
  `rel-dev-w6-selftest-i1`, `rel-dev-w6-selftest-i2`.

Numbers of these stages are development diagnoses: the placement is the stored one (an upper bound for a hidden whole
gene), rows were drawn among genomes that have a cell on the target gene, one seed per arm.
