# W2: tables of version 0 and their census

- `seeddrift/train/rel/tables/v0_build.py`: tables of genomes x loci from a passed selfsup run's training arrays and
  homolog rows (numpy only): one examined genome, a random target gene, its two neighbours, 16 relation genes; other
  rows drawn among the training genomes with a homolog row on the target gene. Shards are plain `.npy` files in the
  fields of the contract. Bit scores are dropped before anything is read; no draw depends on the order of homolog rows.
- `seeddrift/train/rel/tables/v0_census.py`: the census counted while the tables are built (filled cells by group and
  block role, nearest-row identity per block, the fill if rows were drawn at random, codons per group).
- Stages `w2_v0` and `w2_census` (`ci/stages/rel/w2_v0.py`, `ci/stages/rel/w2_census.py`; rules in
  `ci/rules/rel_w2.smk`), profile `rel-dev-w2-v0-i1`. Gates G1 and G2 are decided on the training genomes alone; the
  sealed check runs before any genome is opened; the sealed test split is never read.
- Tests `tests/ci/test_rel_w2_tables.py` (numpy only, a synthetic run with true correspondences) and
  `tests/ci/test_rel_w2_stage.py` (the two stages on that run).
