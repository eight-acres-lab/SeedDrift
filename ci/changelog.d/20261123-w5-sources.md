# W5: stage w5_sources, what arms S1 and S2 take from their sources (a diagnosis)

- Readout `seeddrift/train/rel/readouts/w5_sources.py` (model interpreter, CPU): an arm of the source module built
  from its training's conf.json, the moving average of final.pt, the held-out tables of its final evaluation rendered
  as the evaluator renders them; per scored cell the weight of no source, the entropy of the source weights, the
  levels of the substitution, the identity of the largest source, the bits, and for S1 the weight of no source given
  the true codon. The cells read are checked against the training's final evaluation (the evaluator's digest).
- Stage `w5_sources` (kind rel_traineval, CPU, GPUs hidden): the cells summed by stratum of the nearest identity and
  by block, beside the bits each arm and the reference arm gain from relatives (the final result rows). Profile
  rel-dev-w5-sources-i1 on the early D7 comparison (runs 20261123T110619 and 20261123T122356).
