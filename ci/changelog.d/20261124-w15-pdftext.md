Package W15: `bench/rel/registry/pdf_text.py` (version 0.2), a standard-library PDF text extractor for any PDF
supplement, and stage `w15_registry` item 7.
- What it reads: plain objects and object streams (one broken object leaves the others), the filters FlateDecode, LZWDecode,
  ASCIIHexDecode, ASCII85Decode and RunLengthDecode with the PNG and TIFF predictors (any other predictor is an error;
  DecodeParms padded to the filters), and the text operators Tj, TJ, ', " also inside form XObjects. The font is part
  of the graphics state: q and Q save and restore it, a form starts with its caller's font.
- No guessing: characters come only from what the file states (ToUnicode, kept as ranges; a named encoding with its
  Differences, glyph names by the Adobe rules; MacRoman as the PDF standard defines it, without the 15 Mac OS codes
  it leaves out; Standard encoding for the non-symbolic standard 14 fonts, never for an embedded subset); any other
  code comes out as U+FFFD.
- Limits: 64 MB decoded per stream, 256 MB per document; Flate in 64 KB pieces, a failing piece fed byte by byte to
  find the break; a broken stream makes its page unreadable (corrupt data can decode to wrong text before zlib
  notices), data cut short keep their text with a note; the LZW table stops at 4096; per page 5 million tokens, 2,000
  form calls and 2 million characters, per document 50 million tokens; decoded streams cached by object number.
- A page gives text only when at least 0.95 of its characters are readable and, with 20 or more letter words of four
  letters or more (sequences aside), at least 0.90 of them contain a vowel or a non-ASCII letter, the vowels make
  0.30-0.55 of their ASCII letters and at least 0.45 of their letter pairs are among the 100 commonest English pairs
  (real text measured at 0.72 or more over any 20 to 49 words, every letter shift of it at 0.36 at most).
- Every string from the file written outside the page text has its control characters escaped; in the page text,
  control characters are escaped and lines starting with '#' or '\' get a leading '\', so no file can forge a line.
- Stage item 7 (`pdf_text: {from_run, files}`) reads only PDFs of a run that passed with gate G4_rel_w15_registry
  passed, whose report is the output its stage recorded; each file must match the recorded sha256, and a stored
  supplement archive is checked against its own sha256 before its members are listed. The gate fails on a file not
  stored, ambiguous, changed or not read, and on an archive missing, changed or not opened; an unreadable PDF is a
  result, not a failure. Profile `rel-dev-w15-pdftext-i1`; request `configs/rel/requests/20261124-w15-pdftext.yaml`.
