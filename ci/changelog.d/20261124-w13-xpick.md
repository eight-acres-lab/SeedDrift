# W13: the supplement of eukaryotic viruses and plants and algae (stage w13_xpick; an NCBI Datasets path of fetch and corpus)

- Stage `w13_xpick` (`ci/stages/rel/w13_xpick.py`; kind rel_corpus): the eukaryotic viruses and plants and algae of a
  passed supply run (stage w13_supply, its rules made again by `virus_rows` and `plant_rows`) that stage train_data's
  own `three_way()` puts in a training or held-out unit, by name, and the corpus spec that adds them; every corpus genome
  must keep its split, with and without the candidates of the test units, and none of those is listed. Viruses: the
  ENA record of the GenBank assembly (its sequences become `ena_sequences`; genes called by pyrodigal as for every raw
  virus); plants and algae: `annotated_cds` from NCBI Datasets, at most 1,500 genes. At most 5% of the viruses may be
  unavailable (gate G4_rel_w13_xpick); the new ids pass the sealed check and the exclusions by lineage.
- Stage fetch: source field `ncbi_datasets` ({accession, include: [CDS_FASTA, SEQUENCE_REPORT]}), the v2 genome
  download of NCBI Datasets kept as the zip file it is (no genome sequence), checked whole (every member's CRC, every
  requested part non-empty; a damaged file asked for twice more; no coding sequences = no record). The sources
  without the field describe and download as before (their fetch index keys do not change).
- Stage corpus: an `annotated_cds` source whose file is such a zip file is read by `ncbi_cds()`: pseudogenes,
  organelle sequences (by the sequence report) and another genetic code left out, a gene by its GeneID (else locus
  tag) with its longest coding sequence, then the rules of the Ensembl sources (a length of whole codons, no stop
  codon inside, at least 30 codons) and, with `require_start_stop` (default true), an ATG start and a stop codon at
  the end; the first `max_genes` that pass, in genome order. Every coding sequence left out is counted by reason in
  the corpus manifest (`cds_quality`). The Ensembl sources are read as before.
- Stage w13_build takes a passed w13_xpick run as its pick (`pick_from`): its new genomes are the relatives, without
  anchors.
- The corpus manifest names the rule of each kind of annotated source (meta.cds_rules, only in a corpus with NCBI
  Datasets sources; gene_calling of such a genome says "complete coding sequences only"), and stage w13_build reports
  per new genome the coding sequences left out for want of an ATG start or a stop codon, naming every genome that lost
  more than 20% of its genes that way (rel_w13_build.flag_start_stop_share; the programme lead, 2026-11-24: kept,
  never silently). The Ensembl path does not check start and stop codons; a group can hold both kinds.
