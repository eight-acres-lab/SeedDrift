# W13: the scaffold level of the prokaryotes, metadata only (stage w13_scaffold) and the census's third count

- Stage `w13_scaffold` (`ci/stages/rel/w13_scaffold.py`; kind rel_corpus): NCBI's GenBank assembly summaries of
  bacteria and archaea streamed into the content store (size checked against the server's, sha256 and server date in
  the lock); every scaffold-level assembly (latest version, full representation) judged by the thresholds of its
  profile block: no quality reason among RefSeq's exclusions, at most `max_contigs` contigs, a length within
  `length_band` of its species' complete and chromosome-level assemblies (or `genus_band` of its genus'). Every row is
  kept with its verdict; environmental samples are marked.
- `w13_census` takes `scaffold_from`: a third count, the wide snapshot and the scaffold-level assemblies that pass,
  reported beside the other two.
- `w13_catalog.read` reads a snapshot of another stage in its format.
