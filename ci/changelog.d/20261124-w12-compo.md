# W12: who the training and held-out genomes of a few groups are (stage w12_compo)

- Stage `w12_compo` (`ci/stages/rel/w12_compo.py`; kind rel_tables): for built sets of tables (version 0 or 1) and a
  few groups, every training and held-out genome with species, order, class, kind (land plant or which alga; a
  fungus's phylum; a vertebrate's class), tables as row 0 and split unit, and every held-out genome's nearest training
  genome by taxonomy and by W2's k-mer distance. Reads manifests, indexes, the taxonomy and codon arrays; writes no table.
