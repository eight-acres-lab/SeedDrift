# W3: the strict offline rendering of tables of version 1, and of ruler sets

- Stage rel_w3_offline reads tables of version 1 (32 rows, self-copy rows, ABSENT and TRUNCATED blocks, the fields the
  contract adds, written again as version 1) and ruler sets (allow_kinds: [ruler]; their other files, such as W18's
  meta file and index, copied unchanged under source/, manifest key source_files).
- The manifest is named after its source (w3-strict-<source>-s<seed>, no longer w3-strict-v0) and takes the source's
  kind, purpose and group names.
- New key processes: the tables of a part rendered by that many forked processes; the fourth test's random codons are
  drawn per table, so the output is the same whatever the number.
- Profile rel-dev-w3-offline-v1-i1 (round 1's gate): the held-out tables of version 1, three parts, six processes; its
  tables run and manifest checksum are filled in once rel-dev-w12-v1-k3-i1 has passed.
