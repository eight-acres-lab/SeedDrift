# W18: protocol w18-v3 version 4 in the readout

- The source shift keeps an undefined table (w18-v0's threshold) apart from one that could not be computed (a missing
  pass, a non-finite mean): source_shift_top ranks the first at the top and leaves the second out (nan);
  source_shift_defined (1, 0, nan) feeds the first verdict's defined share.
- The first verdict leaves out near-tier donors (tiers 0 and 1) and reads gain_rel_aa on that population.
- The second verdict (second_verdict): source_shift_top_aa on a population the ruler fixes (tier_span, no self-copy
  rows), the shift and definedness terms per triple, the coverage_shift bar on the cells of passes T and C as rendered
  (w18_passes.shown_cells on the offline placements for a strict reading), labels, failures counted, the controls,
  best_similarity, the primary subset and strata beside it; both verdicts counted per reading.
- One untrained control for every arm (setting untrained when a reading has several); no pairing by name.
