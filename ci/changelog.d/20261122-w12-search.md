# W12: the uncapped search (stage w12_search)

- `seeddrift/train/rel/tables/v1_hits.py` (numpy only): the rows of the uncapped search per query genome: every pair
  (query gene, target gene) once, all its alignments (coordinates and CIGAR), ordered by genome and gene, never by
  score; no score, e-value or rank is stored. A parser for DIAMOND's rows, a merge, a reader, a soundness check.
- Stage `w12_search` (`ci/stages/rel/w12_search.py`, rule in `ci/rules/rel_w12.smk`): the training genomes as the
  database, the training and held-out genomes as queries, every held-out genome also against its own proteins (so every
  genome's other copies are found); both lists through the sealed check first; DIAMOND `-k 0 --max-hsps 0`. A sample
  run (`sample`) searches a share of the query proteins per group and extrapolates time, rows and disk linearly, with
  DIAMOND's fixed cost per call measured by a probe; a full run stops at `wall_s` and resumes from its finished calls.
  Profile `rel-dev-w12-trial-i1`: the 1% trial. `ci/stages/pairs.py` is not touched.
- With `islands: true` (task breakdown 8.8) the island set's development chromosomes that a passed run's island stage
  prepared are extra queries (split "island"): each accession through `common.development_chromosomes` and the sealed
  check, each file against the island manifest, searched against the training database and their own proteins; queries
  only, never rows of a training table. The 1% trial includes them.
