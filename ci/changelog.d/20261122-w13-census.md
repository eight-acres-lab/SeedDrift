- Stage `w13_census` (`ci/stages/rel/w13_census.py`, rule in `ci/rules/rel_w13.smk`; kind rel_corpus): from the
  candidate and selected tables of the selection run the corpus was emitted from (checked against the corpus spec's

  per anchor, and the catalogue lock's record counts beside the totals of its broader queries. Lists only: nothing is
  downloaded, no rule is added, the sealed test genomes are not read. Profile `rel-dev-w13-census-i1`.
