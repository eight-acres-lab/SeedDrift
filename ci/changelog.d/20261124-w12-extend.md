# W12: a search extended to a larger corpus is the full search (rel_w12_search.extend_from, dbsize, algo)

- Stage `w12_search`: `extend_from` (a passed full search of an earlier corpus whose genomes are all here with the
  same splits and corpus files, its rows files checked against its manifest) keeps its rows (target genomes renumbered),
  searches its genomes against the new training genomes only and the new genomes against the whole database, and every
  held-out genome's own proteins again, one genome a call. The extension fixes what makes the result the search done
  again in full: `dbsize` (DIAMOND --dbsize: every e-value against the extended search's database letters), `algo` 0
  and one held-out genome per own-protein search. `verify_proteins` (default 2,000) genes of the earlier genomes are
  searched again against the whole database before the long calls and compared after them, alignment by alignment and
  in order; any difference stops the stage. A search without these settings runs as before.
- `v1_hits.flat` / `combine` / `gene_rows`: rows remade from parts as a Collector makes them, vectorized.
- Test `test_rel_w12_extend.py`: extended and full searches equal byte for byte (rows, genome list, lookup index),
  with the stand-in for DIAMOND and, where it is installed (the CI environment), with DIAMOND.
- Found with DIAMOND 2.2.8 on the fixture: a weak gapped alignment can depend on the other queries of its call, so two
  searches cut into calls differently are not the same pair by pair (none of --ext-chunk-size, --ext full,
  --gapped-filter-evalue 0, --algo 1 removes it). The search's report says so. Round 2 searches in full with round 1's
  settings (the programme lead); the extension stays, to measure that difference before a later extension.
