# W13 step 2: the first batch of close relatives (stages w13_pick and w13_build)

- Stage `w13_pick` (`ci/stages/rel/w13_pick.py`; kind rel_corpus): the relatives of the census's wide count by name,
  up to k per corpus genome of the train and held-out splits (the same species first), each pick from the ENA
  snapshot looked up in its ENA assembly record (a prokaryote must list a chromosome, a virus a replicon, a relative of
  a plasmid anchor a plasmid, whose plasmids become the relative) and replaced by the next candidate when it fails;
  eukaryote relatives with the Ensembl fields of the selection's snapshots. The counts must equal the census run's;
  the new ids pass the sealed check and the exclusions by lineage. It writes the corpus spec that adds the relatives
  (no near-relative cluster: a relative joins its anchor's unit by genus, so no unit of the corpus changes).
- Stage `w13_build` (`ci/stages/rel/w13_build.py`): its rule asks for the corpus stages' outputs, so the run builds
  the corpus of the new spec with the stages as they are; then it checks the pinned splits (every genome of the
  pinned index keeps its split), every relative in its anchor's split, no new training genome in a held-out or test
  unit, the sealed check, and reports the genes of the new genomes and the disk.
- `w13_census`: the wide count also returns the anchors with their candidate rows (the pick's input).
- `w13_build` runs in parts (`rel_w13_build.upto`: fetch, search, all; the rule asks for that stage's output): the
  downloads alone, the search up to its hit files, or everything with the checks. `python -m ci.stages.rel.w13_build
  part I N CORPUS_YAML RELATIVES_JSON` writes the corpus spec of parts 1..I of N (the relatives in N parts of about
  equal proteins), so the search of the new genomes takes N runs of a few hours, each reusing the earlier per genome.
