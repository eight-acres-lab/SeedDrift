# W13: what eukaryotic viruses and plants and algae could still gain (stage w13_supply)

- Stage `w13_supply` (`ci/stages/rel/w13_supply.py`; kind rel_corpus): NCBI genome reports of complete viral
  assemblies and of annotated plant and algal assemblies (the catalogue fetcher of stage catalog; locked with query,
  records, sha256 and date), metadata only. It counts the eukaryotic viruses the selection's rules would take that the
  corpus lacks (by family and genus, genes from the annotation or the length; those the host rule leaves out apart),
  the plant and algal species the corpus lacks (one annotated assembly each, by level, order and family; the CDS
  download estimated), the split each would take under the corpus's rule (including those the test part would take),
  and the groups' current genomes and genes. The user decides any download.
