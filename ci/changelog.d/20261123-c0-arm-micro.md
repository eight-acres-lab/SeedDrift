# Coordinator: an arm's micro-batch in the profile

- `rel_w6_fit.arms[].micro` (1 to the variant's micro-batch) replaces the variant's micro-batch for that arm, as
  `arms[].lr` replaces its rate: the tables per step stay the same, fewer run at once, less GPU memory. The index records
  it (`info.micro_profile`) beside the micro-batch the preflight kept (`info.micro`), and the rows carry the one trained
  with. Round 1's batch 1 on i-1 trains at 1 (`rel-r1short-i1`, `rel-r1probe-i1`): at the variants' 2 the three arms did
  not fit beside another user's processes under the programme lead's margin rule (sum of the preflight peaks x 1.1 + 6 GB).
