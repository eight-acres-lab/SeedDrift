# P0: protocols w18-v0 and w3-offline-v0

- `configs/rel/protocols/w18-v0.yaml`: the pre-registered protocol of the HGT capability check on implant tables
  (package W18), committed before any readout is read: the triples (implant, original, own other gene) with common rows,
  coverage and length rules, the passes, the readouts and their amino-acid versions, the controls, the paired win rate
  with a two-level bootstrap over split units, readability after exclusions, the rule 7 coverage table, the later
  scenarios with dates, and the diagnosis on the 24 development chromosomes of the island set. A ruler: never trained on,
  never an item of a round, never used to select anything.
- `configs/rel/protocols/w3-offline-v0.yaml`: the strict offline rendering of evaluation items under the leak-free
  layout (package W3): the numpy aligner and its scoring, the frame relative for a hidden whole gene, the fourth validity
  test, the two controls after placement, and the layouts of round 1b's training.
