Package W6 (with the minimal trainer of W4): the reference trunk and its training.

- `seeddrift/train/rel/models/axial.py`, `models/common/layers.py`: the axial trunk (row attention over the whole row
  with rotary position inside a locus on half of every head's dimensions; column attention among the rows of a table
  with a learned "no source" key; no row index; PAD cells are no keys in either attention, the row attention on the
  memory-efficient kernel named explicitly on a GPU), sizes 10m (8 x 256), 30m (12 x 384), 100m (12 x 768);
  self-tests in the module.
- `seeddrift/train/rel/data/`: the trainer's reader over the evaluator's (`evalh/shards.py`), the model's grid on the
  evaluator's placement (`evalh/layout.py`; GAP inside a relative's aligned span, PAD outside it and beyond the frame,
  the token of a block without codons), the sampler, the mask schemes `scattered`, `span`, `whole_gene`.
- `seeddrift/train/rel/objectives/mlm.py`, `trainer/`: bidirectional fill-in; warm-up over 1,000 steps, then a
  constant learning rate to the wall limit; evaluation by the moving average of the weights at 1,000, 2,000, 4,000 ...
  tables seen and at the end, under the evaluator's conditions, strata and rows (`evalh/conditions.py`, `score.py`);
  a preflight of 200 steps as the divergence guard.
- Stages `w6_selftest`, `w6_fit`, `w6_report` of kind `rel_traineval` (`ci/rules/rel_w6.smk`): the torch self-tests in
  the model interpreter with the GPUs hidden; every arm preflighted, then trained with the stage's own scheduling (a
  failed arm does not stop the others); the curves at equal tables seen beside the classical baselines.
- Variants `configs/rel/variants/w6-ref10m`, `w6-ref30m`, `w6-ref30m-norel`, `w6-ref100m`; profiles
  `rel-dev-w6-selftest-i1`, `rel-dev-w6-selftest-i2`.

Numbers of these stages are development diagnoses: the placement is the stored one (an upper bound for a hidden whole
gene), rows were drawn among genomes that have a cell on the target gene, one seed per arm, learning rates not tuned.
