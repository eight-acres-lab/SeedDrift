# W18: protocol w18-v3 (the counting reading on round 1's arms)

- Stage w18_offline (new; CPU, kind rel_tables, reads ruler): the strict offline rendering of a ruler set. For every
  table and mask family of the passes (T, C, H1, H2, W) the other rows are placed by W3's aligner (align.strict;
  align.frame_layout for the whole target), and W3's fourth validity test runs on each; a failure stops the stage.
  Outputs data/rel_w18/offline/ (placements and offline.json); gate G4_rel_w18_offline.
- w18_passes: run_ruler(..., offline=<run>) renders a leak-free reading on those placements with W3's leak-free
  renderer (a leak-free reading without them is refused); a presence pass keeps its base pass's layout and shows the
  other rows' codons as MASK; take() renumbers frame_row; the per-cell file records the placements (offline,
  offline_sha256); abstention is read on the presence passes too.
- Implant tables: self-copy rows (rel_w18_rulers.self_copies, version 1 only; table version w18-implant-3), rows
  1..m of A itself, the same number in the three tables of a triple, no distance tier (W18 meta row_tier -2, meta
  self_rows); with self_copies 0 the tables are those of w18-implant-2, draw for draw.
- Readout: one rendering per reading (recorded as layout), arms labelled by their weights (arm@node@N), the relatives
  layers (other-genome rows, self copies only, none) as strata, the criterion's paired share differences
  (criterion.tsv) and the two verdicts per arm and block (report.json verdicts, report.md).
