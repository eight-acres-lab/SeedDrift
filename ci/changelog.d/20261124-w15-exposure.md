Package W15: stage `w15_registry` item 6, the exposure scan. With `exposure_roots` (and `source`) the stage scans names
only, never file contents (bench/hard/V2_REGISTER.md, Scans): every file and folder under the roots is matched by its
own name, as `register.scan_names` does, against the assembly numbers (GCA and GCF spellings, with or without version)
and, for genomes the corpus does not name by assembly, the species binomials of the train and held-out genomes inside
each exclusion radius, and against the record's exclusion taxon (genus or lower) and the binomials of its recipient
column (inside brackets too; `Genus sp.` gives none; `Candidatus` dropped). Levels by path from
`register.path_level` (L3, L4 under a score folder, L5 under `eval/`).
- The sealed test split: a path naming a genome outside the train and held-out splits anywhere, by its assembly number,
  its id's words or its species words (group prefix and strain dropped: `plants_algae_nicotiana_attenuata` also
  matches `Nicotiana_attenuata.*`), is skipped and counted in total only, and such a genome's folders are not entered.
  Walk errors keep only the errno name and the path relative to the root, and an error on such a path is counted only;
  every example and error is checked once more before it is written. No taxon of those genomes is looked up.
- Roots are absolute folders or `{path, prune}` with folders pruned by their path relative to the root; the report
  gives per root the paths walked, the folders pruned, prune entries not found, paths skipped, errors and a sha256 of
  the sorted path list; per record the corpus genomes in the radius, the tokens, the declared level, the scan's level
  (L3-L5, `no_hit` or `no_tokens`) and the higher of the two; declared values that are no level are flagged, and
  scan levels above the declared level are listed.
- The gate fails on a missing root, a root with no path walked, any walk error, or a scan stopped at
  `exposure_max_paths`. Profile `rel-dev-w15-exposure-i1`; request `configs/rel/requests/20261124-w15-exposure.yaml`.
