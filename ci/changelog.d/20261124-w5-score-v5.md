# W5: the evaluator's strata of version 5 (score.VERSION 5)

- A row counts for the nearest identity only when it covers at least half of row 0's stored target block
  (score.MIN_COVERAGE 0.5): score.row_coverage(n, col) is the codons of its target-block segment with a column in row 0
  (those its stored identity is taken over) over row 0's stored codons there. The identity itself is unchanged.
- Tables whose other rows all cover less fall in nearest_identity=short_fragments_only (score.FINE_SHORT), placed
  before nearest_identity=none (score.FINE_NONE, still the last of FINE); not merged with the tables without relatives.
- score.strata_of needs coverage (a ValueError without it); nearest_identity and fine_stratum without coverage read
  as version 4 did (the census of the tables and W18's implants until their protocol says otherwise).
  score.nearest_coverage and score.coverage_by_stratum give the median coverage of the nearest row per stratum, the
  column the reports now carry beside the bins (w5_baselines, w5_sources).
- Unchanged: RELATIVES (some other row has a cell at the target locus, of any coverage), OTHER_ROWS, RELATIVE_KINDS,
  the self-copy rule, the headline and what relatives are worth over all tables.
- The callers pass coverage: the trainer's held-out evaluation (loop.strata), the baselines' result rows, w6_report's
  held strata, w5_sources.
- Why (the programme lead's ruling of 2026-11-24): on the held-out tables of version 1, 12 of the 32 tables of the top
  bin had as nearest row a fragment of one to three codons of a remote homologue at the end of the target block
  (identity 1/1); under version 5 they leave it (32 to 20), and 140 tables are short fragments only. Reports already
  written are not re-run; their tables by bin say "version 4, the 0.9-1.0 bin mixed with fragments" where used.
