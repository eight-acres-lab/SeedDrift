# W18: the readout page names its tables and rendering

- The status line of rel_w18_readout follows the reading: the counting reading (strict offline, protocol w18-v3), an
  adapted diagnosis or the stored layout on implant tables of version 1, or the preliminary reading of version 0 (an
  adapted reading of version 1 no longer carries the sentence about tables of version 0). On a reading other than
  the counting one, protocol w18-v3's criterion is headed as a diagnosis.
