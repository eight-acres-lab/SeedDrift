names only, never file contents (bench/hard/V2_REGISTER.md, Scans): every path under the roots is matched against the
assembly numbers (GCA and GCF spellings, with or without version) and, for genomes the corpus does not name by
assembly, the species binomials of the train and held-out genomes inside each exclusion radius, and against the
record's exclusion taxon (genus or lower) and recipient binomial. Levels by folder from `ci/stages/register.py`
`path_level` (L3, L4 under a score folder, L5 under `eval/`); the report gives per record the declared level, the
scan's level, the higher of the two, paths per level, matched tokens and example paths, with the roots, the paths
walked, the folders not entered and the walk errors. Paths naming a genome outside the train and held-out splits are
skipped and counted in total only (their taxa are never looked up). The gate fails on a missing root or a scan that
stopped at `exposure_max_paths`. Profile `rel-dev-w15-exposure-i1`; request `configs/rel/requests/20261124-w15-exposure.yaml`.
