# W6: round 1 on tables of version 1 (protocol round1, version 2)

- `whole_gene_layout: frame`: the whole gene laid on its frame relative by package W3's strict aligner
  (`tables/align.py` `frame_layout`), after the rows are drawn, then rendered leak-free; the evaluation lays it the
  same way where it renders online. No pair index needed (the online placement by W12's stored pairs stays for
  `whole_gene_layout: leakfree`).
- Evaluation: with `eval_online` and a strict offline rendering (`heldout_offline`), the nodes render online (result
  rows `layout` `leakfree-online`) and the end reads the offline rendering (`leakfree`); the controls are keyed
  `<condition>@<control>` (rows `leakfree+shuffle`, ...). `only_conditions` reaches the trainer from `rel_w6_fit`.
- Nodes also every `nodes_every` tables from `nodes_every_from` on (the long arms: 50,000 from 100,000);
  `keep_node_weights` keeps the moving average of every node as `ema_tables<n>.pt`.
- `data_workers`: the examples drawn ahead by background processes of torch's data loader (the same examples, a
  step's draw depends on seed and step only); the trainer's waits are logged.
- The mask schemes' realised shares of the target cells and of the loss (`steps.csv` `cells_<scheme>`, summary
  `schemes`); the preflight measures the sampler's cost (seconds per step, per table laid by the aligner) and tables
  per second.
- `rel_w6_fit.preflight_only`: every arm preflighted on real tables and nothing trained (the round's 50-step probes).
- Variants `w6-ref30m-v1`, `w6-ref30m-norel-v1`, `w6-ref100m-v1` as the protocol fixes them (no `genome_of_row`
  bias; micro-batches 2, 2, 1); `w6-ref10m-v1` removed (10 M is not run in round 1).
- `rel_w6_eval`: an arm entry may name the weights it reads, `{name, weights: final | best | node@<tables>}` (folder
  `<arm>.<weights>`, rows `ema@best` / `ema@tables=<n>`, a readout's `info.weights` as given; one arm listed several
  times); `best: all` reads the best weights of every arm beside its final ones; `tables.heldout_offline` and
  `end_controls` name the strict offline rendering and the controls in place of the training's own; a readout never
  reads an offline rendering.
